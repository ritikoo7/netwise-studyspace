# 🌐 NetWise | Computer Networks Studyspace

### 🧠 Learn Smarter. Revise Faster. Master Networking.

**NetWise** is an interactive Computer Networks study dashboard designed to make learning, revision, and study planning easier. It brings your syllabus, notes, flashcards, tasks, and focus sessions together in one organized workspace.

🎯 **One dashboard. Five modules. A smarter way to study.**

---

## ✨ What Makes NetWise Special?

📊 **Your Learning Command Center**  
Track your study progress and see how much of your syllabus you've completed.

📚 **Smart Syllabus Tracker**  
Organize Computer Networks topics and monitor your learning journey module by module.

🔄 **Revision Mode**  
Keep important topics organized so you can revisit them before exams.

🃏 **Flashcards for Active Recall**  
Turn revision into a quick question-and-answer session to test your understanding.

🤖 **AI Study Buddy**  
Generate prompts to use with AI assistants for explanations, examples, and study support.

🎥 **Learn Through Videos**  
Search YouTube for topic-specific explanations without manually searching for every concept.

⏱️ **Focus Timer**  
Structure your study sessions and build better study habits.

📝 **Personal Notes**  
Keep important concepts, definitions, and revision points in one place.

✅ **Task Manager & Calendar**  
Plan your study goals, organize tasks, and manage upcoming academic work.

🌙 **Dark Mode**  
Switch to a darker interface for a more comfortable study experience.

💾 **Backup & Restore**  
Export your saved study information and import it later when needed.

📱 **Responsive Design**  
Use the dashboard across supported desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.

## 🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

NetWise uses browser `localStorage` to save supported study information.

- Your saved information stays in the browser where it was created.
- Data may not automatically appear in another browser or device.
- Clearing browser data may delete your saved information.
- Use the backup feature when available to preserve your study data.
- The AI Study Buddy prepares prompts for external AI assistants. It does not itself provide an integrated AI service.

## 💡 Why I Built NetWise

Studying Computer Networks involves more than reading notes. Students also need to track syllabus completion, revise important concepts, practice recall, and manage their time.

NetWise brings these activities together in a single study workspace.

The goal is simple: **spend less time organizing your studies and more time understanding the concepts.**
